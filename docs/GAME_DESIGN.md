# TRADECRAFT Game Design Document

## Core Philosophy

TRADECRAFT operates under the principle: **"A game where learning how markets work IS the gameplay."**

## MarketVerse World Map

The 3D Voxel World consists of 8 distinct thematic destinations:

1. **Beginner Village:** Spawn location, AI Mentor introduction, asset fundamentals.
2. **Market City Hub:** Central trading plaza, NPC traders, order desk instructions.
3. **Stock Exchange Tower:** High-rise financial skyscraper, live simulated indices.
4. **News Tower:** Media headquarters triggering breaking economic news (Crude Oil Surge).
5. **Learning Forest:** Educational hub for technical patterns, volume, support/resistance.
6. **Risk Mountains:** Peak dedicated to drawdown simulation and position sizing.
7. **Psychology Caverns:** Underground analysis center tracking emotional trading habits.
8. **Portfolio Castle:** Majestic vault storing lifetime P&L, badges, and unlocked skills.

## Controls

- **Movement:** WASD or Arrow Keys
- **Sprint:** Left/Right Shift
- **Jump:** Spacebar
- **Interact:** Key [E] when within proximity of NPCs
