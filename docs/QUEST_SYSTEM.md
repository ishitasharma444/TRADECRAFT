# TRADECRAFT Quest & Mission System

## Story Mission 1: "The Market Awakens"

- **Step 0:** Spawn at Beginner Village -> Meet AI Mentor [Press E].
- **Step 1:** Travel to News Tower across MarketVerse.
- **Step 2:** Analyze Breaking News Event (`CRUDE OIL PRICES SURGE 7%`).
- **Step 3:** Enter Stock Exchange Tower & execute a simulated order in OIL or GLD.
- **Step 4:** Review Decision Replay & claim +500 XP and +₹10,000 reward cash.
